Confidentiality

Privacy.

How the maison treats a name, a bag, an address, and a payment. Written for this house.

Who we are

HAYUGEN is a fashion maison designed in Yerevan. This storefront is the house’s own salon. Questions about a file go to the private desk.

The account

A file holds the name and email you give, a hashed password, and the orders placed with that email. The session is an httpOnly cookie. You can sign out from the account page. You may ask to see, correct, or close the file through the account page or the desk.

The bag

Pieces you add are kept with your session so the bag survives a refresh. A guest bag is not tied to a name until you sign in. Quantities can be changed or removed before payment.

Cards and payment

Card charging is not connected. Checkout does not ask for a card number, expiry, or security code, and a new order stays unpaid until a payment processor is connected. A return to this site does not mark an order paid. Older orders may still show a card brand and last four digits from before this change. The full number was never stored.

Dispatch

An order needs a name, an email, a street address, a city, and a country so a piece can leave Yerevan by private courier. Timing is confirmed after the order exists. Duties, where they apply, are the client’s. A phone number is optional and used only to reach you about that dispatch.

Returns

Unworn pieces, with all marks intact, may be returned within fourteen days of delivery. The private desk will arrange the reverse dispatch. Write through the contact page with the order reference from your account.

What else is kept

Notes sent to the desk are stored with the name and email you typed. The house does not sell the file. You may refuse editorial notes without losing the ability to buy.
